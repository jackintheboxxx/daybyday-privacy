1. In brief

DaybyDay does not collect your personal data. We have no server to receive it.

Your records are stored locally on your phone. You, and anyone with access to your unlocked phone, can view them.

2. What we collect

None. We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, location, device identifiers, usage activity or crash reports.

The app contains no third-party analytics, Google Analytics, Firebase, Facebook SDK or advertising tracking SDK.

There is no account system, so no feature requires sign-in.

3. Where your data is stored

Everything is stored on your phone:

  • Text records, including diaries, weight, symptoms, mood, feeding and appointment dates, are kept in the app's local storage.
  • Photos are copied to the app's private folder. With photo permission, a copy is also saved in the phone's DaybyDay album.
  • If iCloud Backup is enabled on your iPhone, these records may be included in your own backup. That backup is between you and Apple; we cannot access it.

4. Health data

The app records information about pregnancy, periods and postpartum health. This is sensitive data, so we do not access it.

The app does not automatically send these records off your device, to a server or to third parties. Exports and device backups are controlled by you.

The app does not read iPhone HealthKit data.

5. Notifications

All reminders are local notifications scheduled and delivered by your phone without a server. We cannot see which reminders you set or whether they arrive.

Turn off individual reminder categories in the app, or disable notification permission in system settings.

6. Your choices

  • Export: Settings → Data backup → Export all data creates a JSON file. You choose where to save it.
  • Restore: restore from a previously exported file.
  • Delete: use Settings → Reset, or delete the app. Deleting the app also removes its local data.
  • Exported files, photos in your phone album and iCloud backups are not removed when you delete the app. Manage those copies separately.

7. Children

The app is for adult caregivers and may contain a baby's birthday, weight and photos. Like other records, these stay on your device and are not collected by us.

The app does not market directly to children and does not show advertising.

8. About the content

Pregnancy, parenting, food, postpartum traditions and health content is drawn from public information and Chinese traditions. It is general information, not professional medical, nutrition or parenting advice, and cannot replace your care team's judgment.

Due dates, periods and ovulation dates are estimated from your entries. Accuracy is not guaranteed; do not rely on them for contraception or conception.

Contact a healthcare professional directly if you notice unusual changes in your body.

9. Policy updates

If a future feature needs to transmit data, such as sharing records between phones, we will update this policy and seek your consent in the app before releasing it. Data will not be transmitted without your agreement.

10. Contact

For privacy questions, contact us using the support details listed on the App Store or Google Play.

In-app Feedback only saves content on your device. It does not send it to us, so the developer will not receive a message through it.

Terms of use — key points

  • DaybyDay's basic recording features are free; some advanced content may require a subscription. The app is provided as-is, without express or implied warranties.
  • App content is general information, not medical advice. You agree not to make medical decisions based solely on this app.
  • You are responsible for backing up your records. We have no copy and cannot restore them for you.
  • Please do not use the app if you disagree with any of these points.
